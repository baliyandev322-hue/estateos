## 🏢 About EstateOS

**EstateOS** is an institutional-grade PropTech platform built for the Indian real estate ecosystem. By combining verified residential and commercial listings with our proprietary **Property Truth Score™**, automated RERA audit checks, and high-precision financial engines, EstateOS eliminates real estate fraud and accelerates property discovery across Western Uttar Pradesh, the National Capital Region (NCR), and Pan-India.

### Key Capabilities
1. **Property Truth Score™ (0–100 Audit)**: Comprehensive verification evaluating legal title clarity, 13-year non-encumbrance certificates, state RERA approvals, physical site progress, and municipal zoning clearances.
2. **AI Property Matchmaker**: Conversational AI assistant helping buyers find their exact budget, BHK, and locality match within seconds.
3. **Hyperlocal Micro-Market Intelligence**: Accurate per-square-foot historical pricing trends and infrastructure indicators for emerging Tier-2 & Tier-3 corridors.
4. **Institutional B2B SaaS**: White-label multi-tenant CRM for real estate brokers and developers with automated WhatsApp Speed-to-Lead integration and Razorpay automated billing.

## 🛠️ Technology Architecture

- **Frontend & App Framework**: Next.js 14 (App Router), React 18, Tailwind CSS, Lucide Icons
- **Backend & Database**: Node.js, Prisma ORM, Cloud PostgreSQL (Supabase)
- **Financial Mathematics**: Native precision engines for Home Loan EMI, Capitalization Cap Rates, UP Stamp Duty & Registry charges
- **Security & Payments**: Razorpay Webhook HMAC-SHA256 verification, bcrypt password hashing, RBAC (Role-Based Access Control)
- **Crawlers & AI Readiness**: Machine-readable `llms.txt` integration for OpenAI ChatGPT, Anthropic Claude, Perplexity, and Google Gemini
